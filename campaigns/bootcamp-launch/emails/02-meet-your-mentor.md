When you learn data science on your own, one of the hardest parts is that no one checks your work. You finish a project, but you don't know if it's good or what to fix.

That's why every student in the bootcamp gets a mentor.

**What your mentor does**

Your mentor reviews each of your 4 real projects. They look at your work, tell you what is working, and suggest what to improve next. You then use that feedback to make the project stronger before it goes into your portfolio.

**Why it helps**

Feedback helps you understand your own work, not just finish it. When you know why you made each choice, it's easier to explain your projects to other people, including employers.

**If you feel nervous about coding or statistics**

That's very common, and it's nothing to be embarrassed about. You don't need to get everything right the first time. Progress comes from practice and support, one step at a time, and your mentor is there for exactly that.
