# DataPath Academy Brand Voice

Use this guide for every email we write for DataPath Academy.

**Who we write to:** College and university students, plus people moving into data science from another career. Most already have intermediate data science skills. They want a stronger portfolio. Many feel nervous about coding and statistics, especially the career changers.

**How we sound:** A calm mentor who has worked in data science for years. We are patient and explain things simply. We are honest about the hard parts and don't hype them up. We are friendly and encouraging, but never pushy, and we never promise quick results.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm. Honest. Encouraging.**

- **Calm:** No hype, no shouting, no pressure. Short sentences and everyday words.
- **Honest:** Say plainly that learning data science takes real effort. Only state facts we can back up.
- **Encouraging:** Remind readers they can make steady progress with practice and support.

## 2. Words we use

- "Step by step training"
- "Real projects"
- "Your portfolio"
- "Basic to advanced". Many readers already have intermediate skills, so when we use this phrase, explain that the basics are a quick refresher before we move on to harder topics.
- Calm, supportive words such as "practice", "progress", "support" and "your mentor".

**True facts we can state:**

- The bootcamp is 12 weeks long and fully online.
- Students finish 4 real projects using real public datasets.
- We start from Python basics, then cover pandas, SQL, data visualisation and machine learning basics. (Explain terms for readers: pandas is a Python tool for working with tables of data. SQL is a language for getting data out of databases.)
- Every student gets a mentor who reviews their projects.
- Students get a portfolio they can show to employers.

## 3. Words we avoid

- "Guaranteed job" or anything that sounds like a promise of work
- "Limited time" and other pressure phrases
- "We are the best trainers in the world" and any other bragging
- Hype words such as "crush it", "explode your career" or "revolutionary"
- Jargon we don't explain

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Any salary number, or "earn X after the course"
- "Get hired in 12 weeks", "job guarantee" or "guaranteed placement"
- Any job placement rate or hiring percentage
- "No coding experience needed" or "anyone can do it easily"
- "Become an expert" or "master data science" in any short time
- Names of companies our students were hired at, unless we have their permission
- Fake urgency such as "last chance" or "only 2 seats left", unless it is actually true

If you're not sure whether a claim is true and allowed, leave it out.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with:

```
Keep learning,
Ukasha Ahmed, Lead Mentor
The DataPath Academy Team
```
