# Angle A: Changing your career

Subject line ideas for **E1. Enrolment is open** (`campaigns/bootcamp-launch/emails/E1-enrolment-open.md`), written for readers moving into data science from another field.

These follow the brand voice: calm and honest, with no job promises or pressure.

1. **Moving into data science? Enrolment is now open**
2. **A step by step path for your career change**
3. **Changing careers? Build 4 real projects with a mentor**

## Notes

- None of these promise a job, a quick result or a salary. They focus on what the bootcamp gives: a clear path, real projects and mentor support.
- Option 3 is the most specific. It names the 4 projects and the mentor, both facts we can state.
- Best fit: the career-switchers version of E1 (`campaigns/bootcamp-launch/groups/career-switchers/emails/E1-enrolment-open.md`).
